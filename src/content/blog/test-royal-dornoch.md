## The round

The opening holes were steady. I found the fairway more often than not and kept the ball below the hole on the greens. The middle of the round was where it got interesting, with the wind picking up and a couple of loose drives that found the rough.

> Golf is a game of misses. The good rounds are the ones where the misses are small.

## Highlights

- A long putt that dropped on the front nine
- Lunch at the turn with a view of the sea
- One proper bunker escape when I needed it

## Next time

I would take one more club into the wind and trust the putter a little more on the fast greens.
