## The plan

A bounce game with Andy

## What went well

My driving was the highlight, solid and mostly straight all round.

## What didn't

On the 8th I aimed left for safety and still sliced it right. Luckily it stopped just short of out of bounds.

## Next time

Sort out my putting. Too many three-putts cost me shots I’d earned from the tee.

## The hole worth knowing

**The 12th.** The par 3 12th looks like it was lifted straight from Augusta National. I hit a great 8 iron to the middle of the green, then three-putted on the slow surface and walked off with an annoying bogey.

## Conditions

The course was in great shape apart from the greens, which were slow, but that’s hardly a surprise in November.
