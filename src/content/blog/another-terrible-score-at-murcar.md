After a disappointing practice round at Pitreavie during the week, I didn't hold out much hope for Murcar today, and I wasn't disappointed.



The problem at Pitreavie was driving accuracy, and it was the problem again today. I was all over the place off the tee, and the only club that behaved itself was the mini driver. Standing over the ball, I had no real idea what I was doing or where it was going to go. Aim left to play a fade and it went straight into the whins. Aim straight and it sliced into the rubbish.



The putting didn't help either. Long putts were either left well short or raced past, and then I'd miss the one coming back. It's frustrating, because I'm usually great on the practice green. Something to sort out, though I'm not sure what yet.



If golf were the best 12 holes out of 18, I'd be better than scratch. Today was a perfect example: level par through five, then a run of bad holes and a couple of lost balls, and the score soon racked up.



The company was good and the course was as great as always. My game just didn't live up to it.
