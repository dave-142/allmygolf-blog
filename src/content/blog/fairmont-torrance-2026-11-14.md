On 14 November I headed to the Fairmont St Andrews for a game with three folk from Scottish Golf Connect, hosted by Duncan. I'd been to the Fairmont before but had only played the Kittocks, so this was my first time on the Torrance. I was expecting it to be cold, but the weather turned out great, and once we got going it was perfect.

The Torrance doesn't have quite the same views as the Kittocks, as it doesn't sit as close to the sea, but the layout more than makes up for it. There are plenty of interesting holes that make you think about where to put the ball.

I shot 81. My driving was good and my approach play was average, but my chipping and putting were terrible, as they always seem to be. I couldn't get the pace of the greens, my lag putting was poor and I three-putted far too often. I didn't get up and down once all day, so every missed green cost me at least a bogey.

The only real downside was the pace of play. We were stuck behind a four-ball who wouldn't let us through, even though they had nobody in front of them. Still, it was a fun game with a really nice group, and it was good to meet Duncan. I didn't play well, but I'd definitely go back.
